ContentBlocks replaces the standard content field on a resource with a visual editor called the **canvas**. Here you build pages from layouts and content blocks your site builder has configured.

[TOC]

## Opening the editor

1. Open a resource in the MODX manager.
2. Find the **Content** area on the edit screen (location depends on your site setup — it may replace the content field or appear in a Content tab).
3. If you don't see the canvas, make sure "Use ContentBlocks" on the Settings tab is enabled.

## Saving

Click **Save** on the resource as usual. Your layout and block content is saved with the page. Unsaved changes may be highlighted (a red indicator on edited blocks) depending on your site configuration.

## What you are editing

- **Layouts** define the column structure of your page (single column, two columns, etc.).
- **Blocks** are individual content pieces (text, images, videos, etc.) placed inside layout columns.

Your site builder controls which layouts and blocks are available. You cannot add new block types yourself — only use what appears in the Add Content modal.
