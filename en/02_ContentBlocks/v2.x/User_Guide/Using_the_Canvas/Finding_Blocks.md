When you click **Insert Block**, a modal opens showing all block types available on your site.

## Categories

Blocks are grouped into **categories** in the sidebar (for example "Content", "Media", "Dynamic"). Click a category to filter the list. The specific available categories are unique to your site.

Blocks without a category appear under **Uncategorized**.

## Search

Use the search box to find a block by name when you know what you are looking for.

## Descriptions

Each block may show a title, icon, and short description to help you pick the right one. If you are unsure which block to use, ask your site builder. They configured which blocks are available for your project.
